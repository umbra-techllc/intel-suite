Sports player props

NFL PropEdge New
Shows what each player will most likely do in passing, rushing, and receiving yards, and the bets most likely to hit, for every game on the week's slate.
  • Projections from models trained on eight seasons of games, including injuries and the backups filling in
  • Picks only on real sportsbook lines, with chances measured on two past seasons
  • Parlay slip that checks how same-game legs affect each other
  • Slate refreshes on its own twice a day
nflverse, ESPN, The Odds APIOpen
NFL TD Predictor New
Shows each player's chance to score a touchdown this week, and how many touchdown passes each starting quarterback is expected to throw.
  • Built on how often each player gets the ball near the end zone
  • Quarterback touchdown-pass trends feed into his receivers' chances
  • Accounts for injuries and missing teammates
  • Touchdown parlay with the chance every leg hits
